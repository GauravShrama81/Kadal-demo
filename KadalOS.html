    Same quarters as the current roadmap

    Q4 26 – Q1 27

    Harden the kernel

    • Program foundation: scoped rules, guidance and reference facts, with drafts confirmed by a person.
    • Integration hub plus the first connectors: LTI 1.3, Common Cartridge and QTI into Canvas, D2L and Open edX; CASE for standards; O*NET and Lightcast for skills.
    • Knowledge graph as a product: one ontology across standards, outcomes and skills.
    • Workflow builder with risk-tiered gates; policy packs and platform checks in the trust loop.
    • Proof in HE: Dallas College Global, first four courses on Open edX.
    Q2 27 – Q3 27

    Make the next build cheap

    • Retrieval and grounding on every source, with citations and a stop when sources are thin.
    • Reuse and consolidation over a seeded, canonical library.
    • Conversational front door that routes a request to the right workflow.
    • Vertical packs: K-12 standards and assessment; workforce skills and pathways.
    Q4 27 – Q1 28

    Scale the packs

    • State editions, adoption correlations and standards refresh.
    • Global editions and localization across all three verticals.
    • Custom agents and tools from partners and clients in the registry.
    • Continuous Publishing runs as one vertical pack on the shared kernel.
