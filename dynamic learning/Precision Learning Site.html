Precision isn't only remediation.

Signal

Both attempts, the exact items missed, and time per question.

Model

Decision

“Let's shore up terminology first. It's what's tripping up your coding accuracy.”

Decision

A short check for the rest of the module, skipping practice he doesn't need.

“You've shown you know this, so take a short check and move on.”

What precision buys.

Higher completion

Effort goes where it's needed instead of being wasted.

Lower cost to serve

Coaches stop answering the same question over and over.

Credential trust

Employers and accreditors get mastery that was actually checked.

A durable advantage

Delivering the right thing is harder to copy than making more content.

Roadmap

Three phases to precision.

Phase 1

Foundation

Tag content by competency, track the interactions that matter, stand up the learner model store.

Phase 2

Adaptive pilots

Ship recommendations in a few programs and compare them against how those programs do today.

Phase 3

Precision at scale

The full decision engine and coach dashboards, across every program.

This is the operating model, not a feature.

Every learner gets the content, pace, and support they need, all the way through.
