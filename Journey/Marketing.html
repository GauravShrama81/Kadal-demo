The challenge

Advisor-led outreach could not watch 21,000 learners in real time. Intervention windows were missed, faculty lacked visibility into performance trends, and learners had little help outside office hours.

What we built

LUCY, a persistent AI assistant inside Canvas LMS, rolled out in three risk-managed phases.

Foundation

Cross-course nudging and in-course support workflows, validated in a controlled environment.

Enhancement

A scalable nudging framework with performance alerts, inactivity detection, SMS and a contextual Q&A assistant across an expanded pilot.

Scale

Moved from a single node to distributed, horizontally scalable infrastructure supporting 21,000 concurrent users across 750 courses.

Impact

  • Advisor response time cut from days to real time.
  • Fewer advisor bottlenecks, with self-service support outside office hours.
  • Faculty query performance and trigger targeted cohort messaging straight from a dashboard.
