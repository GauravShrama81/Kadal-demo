How we build it

Built from your outcomes. Kept live every term.

No publisher content goes in. Every course is written from SNHU's own outcomes and standards, checked before a person sees it, and approved by your faculty.

What goes in: SNHU's own
  1. Accreditation standardsNECHE
  2. Program outcomesPLOs
  3. Course outcomesCLOs
  4. Module outcomesMLOs

Plus SNHU's voice, reading level and accessibility rules.

Our build: four steps, two gates
  1. 1
    BlueprintMap each module outcome to the items it needs.
    Chair approves
  2. 2
    BuildWrite to the outcome, in SNHU's voice.
  3. 3
    VerifyAccuracy, alignment, reading level, accessibility.
  4. 4
    ApproveEvery item, before a student sees it.
    SME signs off
What comes out: owned by SNHU
  • ReadingsMLO 2.1
  • Practice setsMLO 2.1
  • Quizzes and item banksMLO 2.2
  • Visuals and flashcardsMLO 2.3

Every item traces back: MLO → CLO → PLO → standard.

Always current, not a one-time build.

  1. SenseWatch outcomes, standards, links, item results and faculty feedback.

Weeks 1-4

Audit

Score the catalog on SNHU's data. Open two Wave 1 cartridges and map every publisher item to an outcome.

Out: replace-first list, audit reports. About $30K.
Weeks 5-12

Pilot

Rebuild MAT 240 first and run one section on owned content in the next 8-week term, alongside the Wave 1 build.

Out: pass rates and student time, side by side.
Months 2-4

Wave 1

Replace readings and practice in the top courses in parallel. Keep simulations on the publisher for now.

Out: courses relink by month 4; savings start in month 5.
Every term

Keep current

Run the loop: re-align to changed outcomes, rewrite the items students miss most, fix links and refresh about 20% a year.

Out: savings that last.

Questions we expect

We have an enterprise AI license. Why do we need you?

A model writes text. We bring the audit, the prioritization, SNHU's guardrails, the SME workflow, LMS relinking and the maintenance engine, all working today.

Will AI content be accurate?

Every item is written from SNHU's own outcomes, not publisher text, checked for accuracy and alignment, and approved by an SME before it is relinked. Nothing goes live unreviewed.

Will accreditors accept it?

Outcomes, alignment evidence and review records are captured for each item, which supports the accreditation file.

Publishers give us platforms and ancillaries.

Keep simulations and labs on the publisher in wave 1. Replace readings and practice first, where most of the spend and student time sits.

Who owns the content?

SNHU does. It sits in SNHU's LMS and repository, with no access codes or per-student fees.

This sounds like a big project.

Start with the paid audit, rebuild one course and pilot it with one section before scaling.

Methodology and caveats
