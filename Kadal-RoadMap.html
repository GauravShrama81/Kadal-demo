    Same quarters as the current roadmap

    Q4 26 – Q1 27

    Harden the kernel

    • Integration hub plus the first connectors: LTI 1.3, Common Cartridge and QTI into Canvas, D2L and Open edX; CASE for standards; O*NET and Lightcast for skills; OneRoster.
    • Knowledge graph as a product: one ontology across standards, outcomes and skills.
    • Workflow builder with risk-tiered gates; policy packs in the trust loop.
    • Proof in HE: Dallas College Global, first four courses on Open edX.
    Q2 27 – Q3 27

    Add the learner layer

    • Unified learner record with one learner ID and consent.
    • Learner experience delivered inside the LMS; Precision Learning pilots.
    • Events and nudges; advisor single view.
    • Vertical packs: K-12 standards and assessment; workforce skills and pathways.
    Q4 27 – Q1 28

    Close the loop

    • Metrics layer defined once; efficacy and content-health signals.
    • Early alert and portfolio intelligence feeding the design tools.
    • Custom agents and tools from partners and clients in the registry.
    • Continuous Publishing runs as one vertical pack on the shared kernel.
