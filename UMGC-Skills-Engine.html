North Star

From content projects to a skills engine UMGC owns

For UMGC
When the job market shifts, UMGC has a program live within a quarter, and every program becomes a way into a UMGC degree.
Why it matters

Job skills change faster than courses get rebuilt. A slow catalog loses learners to faster providers.

What it gives you

A repeatable engine, current content, and data on what each learner can do.

Evidence

Mastery per skill, completion at 100% and 150% of expected time, monthly.

For learners
Every working adult who enrolls finishes with skills they can prove and credit that can count toward a degree.
Why it matters

Time spent on what they already know is the fastest way to make a working adult quit.

What it gives you

A path that skips known skills, help when stuck, and practice on real work.

Evidence

A verifiable badge for each skill, traced back to the learner's own work.

Solution thinking

We redesigned the workflow, not just sped it up

Traditional content development passes work between six roles and repeats reviews at every handoff. We removed the steps that exist only to move work between people, and kept every decision a person should make.

Foundation first

Content is the last thing we build, not the first

Everything starts from an ontology and a skills map UMGC approves. AI drafts each layer, a person approves it at a gate, and only then does the next layer begin. That is why a lesson written for one learner still fits the program.

The learner experience

Brightspace is the front door. The Kadal layer is the room.

Learners sign in, enroll and see grades in Brightspace. Inside it, through LTI 1.3, we run the moments that decide whether someone learns. Scroll through one learner's path in SOC Analyst Foundations.

1Start

The gap is clear before the first lesson

The learner sees their skills against the SOC Analyst I target role. A placement check skips what they already know.

  • Skills from NICE, Lightcast and O*NET
  • Paste a job posting to compare
2Learn

A lesson written for this learner, checked first

Low confidence on a topic gets a short lesson built around the learner's profile and the mistake they made. Every check runs before they see it.

  • Transcripts, chapters and notes
  • AI tutor that cites its sources
3Prove

Practice on real work, scored by fixed rubrics

Scenarios from the target job. Every score points back to what the learner did, and mastery updates per skill.

  • Evidence log saved through xAPI
  • The same rubric for every learner
4Earn

Badges that show what they stack into

Verifiable Open Badges 3.0 for each skill, stacking to a certificate. UMGC decides how a certificate maps to degree credit.

  • Attachment D package ready at launch
5Next

A plan that fits a working week

A study plan around the learner's hours, re-planned when they fall behind, and a next step with its reason.

  • Nudges after 10 quiet days
  • Pauses for deployment and military duty
