Governed by design

AI you can put in front of an adoption committee.

BlocksHard gates

Breaks a policy, gets a fact wrong, cites no source, or is wrong for the grade or audience. It cannot pass until it is fixed.

FlagsSoft gates

Tone drifts, reading level runs high, or the link to the standard is weak. It goes ahead, marked for a reviewer.

Always onChecks

Copyedit, accessibility, alignment to standards and outcomes, and whether the unit reads as one whole.

When a block fails, only that block is rewritten and checked again. If it still fails, reviewed content takes its place and your team is told.

Your rules, as data.

Policies, house style and reference facts apply from your organisation down to a single module. The strictest rule wins. Anything drafted stays a suggestion until a person confirms it.
